Suresign is an agentic workspace. A built-in AI agent works beside you in your workspace. It drafts agreements, places signature fields, sends documents you approve, answers questions, chases signers, and runs automations. Think of it as a signing assistant that gets smarter about your work the more you use it.

## Where to find the agent

The agent is always one click or keypress away.

| Where you are | How to open the agent | What it knows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Click **Ask the agent** at the top of the page, or drop a PDF into the same box | Your whole workspace |
| The Agent page | Open the **Agent** page from the sidebar, then pick **Overview**, **Chats**, **Automations**, or **Activity** | Your whole workspace |
| Beside any document | Press **A** (or **Cmd+J** on Mac, **Ctrl+J** on Windows) | That document |

On a phone, tap the **\+** button in the tab bar to open the agent sheet. The same button also gives you quick links to upload, sign yourself, use a template, or jump to your chats.

## What the agent can do

You type a sentence. The agent does the rest. Here is what that looks like in everyday work.

| What you ask | What the agent does | Example prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft an agreement | Writes a full draft with signatures placed | "Mutual NDA with Northwind Studio, two years, New York law" |
| Place signature boxes | Finds where each person signs in a PDF | Upload your consulting agreement PDF and ask it to prepare the document |
| Send a document | Prepares everything and waits for your approval | "Send the offer letter to Jordan Lee" |
| Answer questions | Explains clauses, shows status, finds deadlines | "What does clause 4 say?" |
| Chase signers | Sends reminders on your schedule | "Remind Priya Shah every 3 days" |
| Extend a date | Pushes the expiry before it passes | "Extend the NDA by one week" |
| Run automations | Repeats tasks like weekly summaries or expiry warnings | "Send me a summary every Monday at 9:00" |

## How it fits with sending and signing

The agent can do almost everything for you: draft the agreement, place the boxes, send it, and follow up. The one thing it never does is sign. Signing is always done by a person: you, or whoever you sent the document to.

| The agent can | Only people can |
| --- | --- |
| Draft and edit the agreement | Sign the document |
| Place signature boxes for each person | Approve the document, if they are an approver |
| Send it, after you approve | Decline to sign |
| Remind people and extend the expiry date | Fill in their own fields |

When you draft with the agent, it writes the agreement beside the chat and places signature boxes for each signer. A card appears that says **Review and send**. Nothing goes out until you press that button.

When you upload a PDF yourself, the agent can still help. Ask it to place the boxes, set the signing order, or remind people later. Every action that reaches another person shows up on a card for your approval first.

That is **Manual**, the mode every chat starts in. On Business, where an admin allows it, you can put a chat on **Auto**: the agent works on autopilot and tells you what it did. See [Manual and Auto](/agent/manual-and-auto).

<Note>
  The agent is available on every plan, including Free. You do not need to turn it on. It is already in your workspace.
</Note>

## Credits keep it running

The agent works on credits. Drafting and sending an agreement takes about 30 credits. Asking a question or sending a reminder takes a few. Every plan includes a monthly allowance: 100 on Free, 1,000 on Pro, and 5,000 on Business. Credits renew on the 1st of each month.

<Note>
  Running out of credits only pauses the agent. Sending and signing never stop, and nothing already sent is affected.
</Note>

Learn more about credits on the [Credits and plans](/agent/credits) page.

<Tip>
  Ask the agent to do something right now. Start with a sentence like "Draft a consulting agreement with Alex Chen and Priya Shah."
</Tip>
