Suresign has a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, so you can use it from your own AI assistant. The assistant acts as you: it sees what you can see, and anything it sends waits for your click on Suresign's card.

## What it needs

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Plans | Every plan. Sending from an assistant needs Pro or higher |
| Assistant | Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code, or any assistant that adds MCP connectors by address, with sign-in |
| Who | Each person connects their own assistant |

## Connect your assistant

Go to **Integrations** > **AI assistants** in Suresign and copy the connector address. It ends in `/mcp`.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Claude">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Connectors">
        In Claude, open **Settings**, then **Connectors**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the connector">
        Choose **Add custom connector**, name it Suresign, and paste the address.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Sign in to Suresign when it asks, and press **Allow**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ChatGPT">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open connectors">
        In ChatGPT, open **Settings**, then **Apps and connectors**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a connector">
        Create a connector with the address. If it asks, turn on developer mode under **Advanced**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Sign in to Suresign when it asks, and allow access.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cursor and Claude Code">
    In Cursor, go to **Settings** > **MCP** > **Add new server**, then paste:

    ```json
    {
      "mcpServers": {
        "suresign": {
          "url": "YOUR_CONNECTOR_ADDRESS"
        }
      }
    }
    ```

    In Claude Code, run this in a terminal:

    ```bash
    claude mcp add --transport http suresign YOUR_CONNECTOR_ADDRESS
    ```

    Each asks you to sign in to Suresign the first time it is used.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Another assistant">
    Any assistant that adds MCP connectors by address, with sign-in, can use Suresign. Add a connector, paste the address, and sign in when asked.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What your assistant can do

- Find documents and say who still has to sign.
- Send from your templates, after you confirm each send (Pro and higher).
- Remind people and move expiry dates, under your workspace's rules.
- Read a document's words when you ask about it, if your workspace allows it.
- Add a PDF or Word file (up to 3 MB) as a draft and place the boxes on it.
- Give you a download link for the signed copy, certificate, or original. The link works for 10 minutes.

It can never sign for you, delete or cancel documents, change settings, or invite people.

### Available tools

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `who_am_i` | Who the connection acts as, the plan, documents left this month, and what the assistant may do |
| `find_documents` | Search documents by title, person, or status |
| `get_document` | Status, people, signing order, expiry, and recent history of one document |
| `list_templates` | The workspace's templates and the roles each one needs |
| `find_contacts` | Look people up by name, email, or company |
| `read_document` | A document's words, page by page |
| `find_text` | Where a word or phrase appears in a document |
| `download_document` | A 10-minute download link for a PDF |
| `upload_document` | Add a file as a draft. Nothing is sent |
| `read_layout` | Where people sign on a draft's pages |
| `prepare_document` | Place people and boxes on a draft |
| `send_template` | Send from a template, after you confirm |
| `send_document` | Send a prepared draft, after you confirm |
| `send_reminders` | Remind people still to sign, after you confirm |
| `extend_document` | Move a document's expiry date later, after you confirm |
| `check_confirmation` | Whether you have confirmed something the assistant asked for |

## Confirm every send

When your assistant wants to send a document, remind people, or move a date, Suresign shows a card in the chat with **Send** and **Not now**. Nothing reaches anyone until you press **Send**. Addresses you have not sent to before must be checked on the card.

If your assistant cannot show the card, open **Integrations** > **AI assistants** in Suresign. Anything waiting appears under activity with **Confirm or decline**.

If your workspace lets reminders and new dates go on their own, those skip the card. See [Staying in control](/agent/staying-in-control).

<Note>
  Nothing can be sent until your email address is confirmed in Suresign.
</Note>

## Manage your assistants

**Integrations** > **AI assistants** lists every assistant you have connected, when it was last used, and what it did. Select **Disconnect** to end its access at once.

## Rules for admins

Admins set rules for everyone's assistants on the same page:

- **Who can connect an assistant:** Everyone, Admins, or No one. Narrowing it disconnects the assistants it leaves out.
- **Read documents' words:** when off, assistants see titles, people, and status, never contents.

## What does not change

- An assistant acts as you, with your role and permissions, never more.
- Every step is recorded in the document's [audit trail](/proof/audit-trail-and-certificate).
- Your documents are never used to train any model.
