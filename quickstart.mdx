You can send your first document in about a minute. Suresign is free for 10 documents a month and no credit card is needed. There are two ways to start: describe what you need to the agent, or upload a file and prepare it yourself. Both get you to the same place: a sealed, signed document with a certificate and proof trail.

If you want to try before you create anything, your dashboard has a **Try a sample NDA** button. It sends you a real two-page sample NDA with a real signing link, so you can see exactly what your signers will experience.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Start with the agent">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign up free">
        Go to [suresign.si](https://suresign.si) and create an account. You can sign in with Google, GitHub, or Microsoft, or use an email address and password. You can also sign in with a magic link sent to your email.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Describe the agreement">
        In the agent chat, type what you need. For example: "Mutual NDA with Northwind Studio, two years, New York law." The agent drafts the text beside the chat and places signature boxes for each person.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review the draft">
        Read the draft that appears. Check the signer names and order. If something is wrong, type "Ask for a change..." and tell the agent what to fix.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review and send">
        When you are happy, click the **Review and send** card. The agent prepares the document but does not send it until you press the button. Every send waits for your click.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Track and collect">
        Watch on your dashboard as people open and sign. Send reminders or ask the agent who still needs to sign. When the last person finishes, a sealed PDF and certificate come to everyone on the document.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      You can also attach a file to the chat and ask the agent to place the signature boxes on it.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Upload a PDF">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign up free">
        Go to [suresign.si](https://suresign.si) and create an account. You can sign in with Google, GitHub, or Microsoft, or use an email address and password. You can also sign in with a magic link sent to your email.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Upload a file">
        Click **New document** and choose **Send for e-signature**. Drop a PDF, Word file, or image into the upload box. When the upload finishes, the file opens in the editor where you can place fields.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Place fields">
        Drag and drop fields onto the document. Choose from 13 types: Signature, Initials, Date signed, Name, Text, Number, Checkbox, and file uploads. The Date signed field fills automatically when the signer finishes.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add signers">
        Enter each person's name and email. You can add up to 50 people. Set the signing order to **One after another** or **All at once**. Add approvers or people who get a copy if needed. Write a personal message for each person if you want.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send">
        Review everything and click **Send**. Each signer gets their own signing link by email. Or ask the agent to help you review before sending.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Track and collect">
        Watch opens and signatures as they happen on your dashboard. Send reminders, extend the expiry date, or resend an expired document. When the last person signs, a sealed PDF, certificate, and signed copy come to everyone.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What happens next

After you send, here is what unfolds:

1. **Signers get an email** with their signing link. They need no account and no app.
2. **They open the link** and, if you asked for checks, enter an access code or a 6-digit emailed code.
3. **They adopt a signature** by choosing a style, drawing, or uploading an image. They click **Adopt and sign**.
4. **The date fills itself** if you placed a Date signed field.
5. **They click Finish** or decline with a reason.
6. **If the order is one after another**, the next person gets their turn automatically.
7. **When everyone is done**, the document is sealed. Everyone gets a signed copy by email, plus a certificate and proof trail.

<Note>
  If your document has an expiry date and someone misses it, you can extend the date or resend the document in one click. You can also ask the agent to remind people.
</Note>
