Integrations send your documents to the apps your team already uses. Documents are signed in Suresign, then go only to the apps you connect. Open **Integrations** from the sidebar to see every app, what it does, and whether it is connected.

## What happens on its own

Recipes are ready-made setups. Pick one, connect its app, and it runs on every new document.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="File every signed copy">
    When any document completes, the signed PDF and its certificate are saved to the folder you pick in Google Drive, Dropbox, Box, or OneDrive. Each file is named with the document's title and the date it completed. You can also turn on a folder for each client, and choose whether the certificate is saved as its own file beside the PDF.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Offer letters that file themselves">
    When a document from the template you pick completes, it is saved in the person's own folder (for example, **My Drive / Candidates / their name**), and a Slack message says who signed with a link to it in Suresign.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tell the team when it's signed">
    When a document completes or is declined, a message in Slack or Teams gives its title, who signed, and a link to it in Suresign. The document itself is never posted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Every document in a sheet">
    When a document is sent, signed, completes, or is declined, its row in Google Sheets, Notion, or Airtable is added or updated with the title, people, status, and dates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Signed copies on the CRM">
    When a document completes, the signed PDF and a note go on each signer's contact in HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, or Zoho CRM, or on their open deal. You can also have the deal moved to a stage you pick once everyone has signed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Start from a deal stage">
    When a deal reaches the stage you pick, Suresign fills in a one-signer template for the deal's contact and puts it in **Needs you** for the admin who connected the CRM. Nothing reaches the contact until someone selects **Send**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Expiry dates on a calendar">
    When a document is sent, an all-day event goes on the day it expires, in Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar. It comes off once the document completes, is declined, or is cancelled. Use one calendar for the workspace, or let each person keep the dates of documents they sent on their own calendar.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

A new connection does nothing until you turn on a recipe or a setting.

## Connect an app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app's page">
    Go to **Integrations** and select the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Select **Connect**. Suresign lists what it will ask the app for before you sign in. Then sign in on the app's own page and approve access. That page may name Composio, the service that connects apps for Suresign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what it does">
    Pick a folder, channel, spreadsheet, calendar, or deal stage, and turn on the recipes you want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each app's page shows its recent activity. If a save or post fails, select **Retry** to try it again.

You can also connect from a chat with the agent. See [Use integrations with the agent](#use-integrations-with-the-agent).

## Who can connect what

- **Admins** connect apps for the whole workspace.
- **Members** can connect their own Google Drive, Dropbox, Box, or OneDrive to pick files when starting a document, and their own Google or Outlook calendar for the expiry dates of documents they sent, once an admin approves that app. Manage your own accounts in **Settings** > **Connected accounts**.
- A member can ask an admin to connect an app, approve it, or turn on a recipe from the app's page.

## Use integrations with the agent

The agent can see your connected apps and what failed in the last week. It can:

- Find PDFs and Word files in your connected storage and start a draft document from one.
- Save a completed document's signed PDF to storage, or post about it in Slack or Teams, right now.
- Turn on a recipe for an app that is already connected (admins only).
- Offer a **Connect** button in the chat when what you ask needs an app that isn't connected. Sign in from there, and the agent carries on when you're back. If only an admin can connect the app, it says so.

Saving, posting, and turning on a recipe wait for your approval, like everything else the agent does that reaches outside Suresign. The agent never connects an app itself. See [Staying in control](/agent/staying-in-control).
