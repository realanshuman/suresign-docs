Sometimes you are the only person who needs to sign. A self-signed letter, a memo, or a one-person affidavit. Suresign handles this just like any other document, with the same proof and protection. This page shows you how.

## When to sign it yourself

Signing alone is useful when:

- You need a signed statement for your records.
- You are the only party to the document.
- You want the same legal proof and verification as a multi-signer document.

Even with no other signers, Suresign gives you a sealed PDF, a certificate of completion, and a hash-chained audit trail anyone can check.

## How to self-sign a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new document">
    Click **New document** and choose **Sign it yourself**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Drop a PDF, Word file, or image into the upload box. When the upload finishes, the file opens in the signing editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your signature">
    Drop a Signature, Initials, Date signed, or Text field onto the page. The first time you place a signature, adopt it by choosing a style, drawing one, or uploading an image. Every field after that lands already signed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Click **Finish**. The document seals immediately because you are the only signer. Nothing is emailed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Suresign does not save a draft while you self-sign. If you leave before you click **Finish**, start again from **New document**.
</Note>

## What you get after signing

After you finish, Suresign creates:

- A **sealed, flattened PDF** with your signature baked in. No one can edit it.
- A **certificate of completion** showing your name, the time, and the document fingerprint.
- An **audit trail** with a SHA-256 fingerprint and hash chain. Each step points to the one before it, so no one can change history.

Anyone can verify this document at [https://suresign.si/verify](https://suresign.si/verify) by uploading the file.

## Reusing your saved signature

After you sign once, Suresign saves your signature for next time. Next time you open a signing link, your saved signature appears. You can reuse it, draw a new one, or upload a different image.

Your saved signatures live in **Settings > Signatures**. You can manage them there anytime.

<Tip>
  Your saved signature is private to your account. No one else can see or use it.
</Tip>

## What the agent can do

You can ask the agent to prepare a self-signed document for you:

- "Create a self-signed memo."
- "Draft a statement of work and leave just my signature."

The agent places the fields, assigns them to you, and shows a "Review and send" card. You press the button when ready.
